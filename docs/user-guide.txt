ABU DHABI GRAND PRIX 2021 - RACE REPLAY
Unofficial 3D reconstruction of the race at Yas Marina, 12 December 2021
======================================================================

WHAT THIS IS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
A replay of the real race, not a simulation. Every car crosses the
finish line at its real time on every one of the 58 laps, so the order
and the gaps at the line are those of the race. The final lap has its
own button and camera sequence: the restart, the pass at Turn 5, the
fight to Turn 9, the crew on the pit wall, the flag and the fireworks.

Unofficial fan-made reconstruction. Not affiliated with Formula 1, the
FIA, the teams or the drivers.


WHAT YOU NEED
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- The file "index.html". The whole app is this one file.
- A current web browser: Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.
- An internet connection when you open it. The page downloads the
  Three.js 3D library from cdnjs.cloudflare.com each time it loads.
- Nothing to install. No account, no server.


OPEN IT
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Double-click "index.html". It opens in the default browser and
starts playing by itself from the starting grid.

For the best view, make the browser window full screen.


THREE WAYS TO WATCH (buttons at the bottom right)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Final lap     Jumps to the end of the safety car period and plays the
              last lap at real speed with the director camera.
              About two and a half minutes.
Highlights    Plays the nine key moments at real speed and fast-forwards
              between them. About 11 minutes.
Full race     From the grid at real speed (1 hour 30 minutes). Use the
              speed buttons to go faster.


CONTROLS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Playback bar (bottom)
    Play / pause              Also the Space key
    1x  4x  16x  60x          Playback speed
    Timeline                  Click or drag to go to any moment. The
                              marks are the nine chapters; click a mark
                              or use the Chapters menu to jump.
    Panels                    Hide or show the timing and detail panels

Camera (top left)
    Map                       Free overview. Follows the selected car.
    Chase                     A camera attached to the selected car, or
                              the leader. View buttons appear next to
                              it: Behind, Front, Left, Right, Above and
                              Onboard (from above the driver's head).
    TV                        Trackside cameras
    Director                  Scripted shots for the final lap
    Camera pad                The small buttons next to the camera
                              buttons: rotate left and right, tilt up
                              and down, zoom in and out, reset. Hold a
                              button to keep moving.

Mouse, in the Map camera
    Drag (left button)        Move the map
    Right-button drag         Rotate and tilt
    Shift + drag              Same as right-button drag
    Scroll wheel              Zoom
    Click a car               Select it; details show on the right
    Click empty ground        Clear the selection

Mouse, in the Chase camera
    Drag                      Look around the car from any side
    Scroll wheel              Closer or further away
    A view button             Goes back to that fixed view

Keys
    Space                     Play / pause
    Q  E                      Rotate left / right
    R  F                      Tilt up / down
    +  -                      Zoom in / out
    0                         Reset the view

See-through structures
    When something stands between the camera and the car you are
    looking at (the start gantry, the bridges over the track, the hotel
    bridge and canopy, a grandstand roof), it turns see-through and
    comes back when it is no longer in the way.

Touch screen / phone
    One finger drag           Move the map
    Two-finger pinch          Zoom
    Two-finger twist          Rotate
    Tap a car                 Select it
    Bottom sheet              Drag the handle to resize it

Top bar
    Search box                Driver, team, camera area or chapter
    Start/finish, Turn 5,     Fly the camera to that part of the track
      Chicane, Turn 9, Hotel
    Bell                      Race log
    Sun                       Daylight: view the scene in afternoon
                              light at any moment of the race
    i                         About this replay: what is real, what is
                              estimated, credits

Panels
    Timing                    Position, gap, interval, last and best
                              lap, tyres and their age, stops. Click a
                              row to select that car. "On track" orders
                              the rows by position on the road (an
                              estimate between lines); "At the line"
                              uses the last real line crossing.
    Pit stops, Race log       Tabs next to Timing
    Title as it stands        Championship points if the race ended now


WHAT IS REAL AND WHAT IS ESTIMATED
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Real, from the published race record:
    every lap time and line-crossing time, positions and gaps at the
    line, pit stop laps and pit-lane times, tyre compounds, grid,
    results, championship points. The lap times add up to the official
    race time (1:30:17.345) and to every official gap.
From race reports:
    where the key moments happened (Turn 6 on lap 1, Perez against
    Hamilton, Giovinazzi stopping at Turn 9, Latifi's crash at Turn 14,
    the pass at Turn 5).
Estimated or illustrative:
    where each car is between two line crossings, speeds, side-by-side
    moves, exact times of the safety car phases, the local clock (to
    about 5 seconds), the look of the buildings, camera views, crew
    celebrations, fireworks.
The cars:
    one shared 2021-style shape for all teams, painted in each team's
    colour pattern from memory of the 2021 season, with the car number,
    the driver's three-letter code and the team name as plain text.
    Helmet colours are illustrative. No team's real bodywork, logos or
    sponsor markings are reproduced.
Not confirmed by a source:
    the safety car colour, the order of the garages along the pit lane,
    the position of the start line to within about 50 metres.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Message "Three.js could not be loaded from the CDN"
    The computer is offline, or the network blocks
    cdnjs.cloudflare.com (some company networks do). Connect to the
    internet or try another network.

The dashboard shows but the 3D view is blank or black
    WebGL is switched off. Turn on hardware acceleration:
      Chrome / Edge: Settings > System > "Use graphics acceleration
                     when available", then restart the browser.
      Firefox:       Settings > General > Performance.

Slow or choppy
    Close other heavy tabs, plug in the charger, make the browser
    window smaller. The scene has about 700,000 triangles; older
    laptops without a graphics chip will be slow.

The page does not start when opened by double-click
    Opening by double-click has not been tested; the page was tested
    served from a local web server. If double-click fails:
      1. Open Terminal (Mac) or PowerShell (Windows).
      2. Go to the folder that holds index.html.
      3. Run:   python3 -m http.server 8000
         (on Windows: python -m http.server 8000)
      4. In the browser open:
             http://localhost:8000/index.html
    This needs Python installed on that computer.
