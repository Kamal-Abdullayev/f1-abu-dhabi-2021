About this replay

A fan-made 3D replay of the race, rebuilt from public timing data. Every car crosses the finish line at its recorded time on every lap, so the order and the gaps at the line are those of the real race. What happens between two crossings of the line is a reconstruction.

Real (from the timing data and results)

  • Line-crossing times, lap times, and the order and gaps at the finish line on every lap.
  • The laps on which cars pitted and the time each stop took in the pit lane.
  • Tyre compound of each stint, grid positions, retirements, the final classification and the points.
  • The timing table: gap, interval, last lap, best lap, tyres and stops are the values at each car's last crossing of the line.

Estimated or illustrative

  • Where each car is within a lap, its speed, its line and the side-by-side moves. Places of the incidents and passes follow race reports.
  • The order "on track" between two crossings of the line, live gaps and speeds. They are marked est. wherever they are shown.
  • The local clock (approximate, within a few seconds), the time spent stationary in the pit box, and the pace under the virtual safety car and the safety car.
  • Camera views, the look of the cars and the circuit, crews, crowd, celebrations and fireworks.
