Honest data

What is real, what is estimated

Real

From the race record

  • Every lap time and line-crossing time
  • Positions and gaps at the line
  • Pit stop laps and pit-lane times
  • Tyre compounds, grid, results and championship points

Reported

From race reports

  • Turn 6 on lap 1
  • Perez against Hamilton
  • The stopped car at Turn 9
  • The crash at Turn 14 and the pass at Turn 5

Estimated

Modelled by us

  • Where a car is between two line crossings
  • Speeds and side-by-side moves
  • Exact timing of the safety car phases, the local clock
  • Buildings, crews, fireworks and camera views

In the app, every estimated figure carries an “est.” label, and an About panel lists all of this.
