Sunday 12 December 2021 · Yas Marina Circuit

58 laps.
19 cars.
One pass at Turn 5.

Two drivers arrived at the last race level on points, for the first time since 1974. Whoever finished ahead would be champion. It was settled on the final lap, and we wanted to watch that lap again from anywhere on the circuit.

Start with why this race. After 21 races the two title contenders were tied, so no maths was needed: whoever finished ahead was champion. One led almost all evening, and it still turned on a single move on the last lap. We built this so we could go back and look at it from any angle.
