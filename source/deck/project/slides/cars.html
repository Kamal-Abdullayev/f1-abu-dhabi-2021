The cars

Built for a close look

2,792 triangles per car, all generated in code. No model files.

One 2021-style shape, ten colour patterns, with car numbers and driver codes.

No logos or sponsor artwork. Team and driver names appear as plain text.

Abu Dhabi 2021 · race replay · unofficial fan project

The cars hold up when the camera gets close: front and rear wings, halo, wheels with coloured tyre walls, helmet. All ten teams share one body shape, painted in each team’s colour pattern from memory of the 2021 season. We chose not to reproduce real logos or sponsor markings; names appear as plain text only.
