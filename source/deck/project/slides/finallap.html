Abu Dhabi 2021 · race replay · unofficial fan project

This is the heart of the project. The outline is our own track model, drawn from open map data in the same orientation as the app. The two dots are an illustration that plays once when the slide opens: they run at steady speed, and the blue one draws level just before Turn 5, as in the replay. In the app each car follows its real lap. The Final lap button plays these four beats with a scripted camera sequence in about two and a half minutes.
