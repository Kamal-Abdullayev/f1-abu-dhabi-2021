Abu Dhabi 2021 · race replay · unofficial fan project

The race started in daylight and ended under floodlights, and the replay does the same: golden hour at the start, sunset around lap 22, night for the finish. The track, the pit lane, the buildings, the marina and the roads come from open map data; the look of the buildings is our own. A Daylight button shows the scene in afternoon light at any moment.
