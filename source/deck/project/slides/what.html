What it is

A replay, not a simulation

The race is stored as data, not video. From the published lap times we work out where every car was at every moment, then draw it in 3D in the browser.

Real at the line

All 998 lap times and 29 pit stops come from the race record.

Estimated in between

Between two line crossings, each car’s position is modelled. The app labels those figures as estimates.

You choose what to watch

Pause it, rewind it, follow any driver from any camera at any point in the 90 minutes.

Abu Dhabi 2021 · race replay · unofficial fan project

The key idea: nothing here is invented racing. A simulation would produce a different race every time. This always produces the race that happened, because every car is tied to its real time at the finish line on every lap. The picture is the hotel section on the last lap, taken from the app.
